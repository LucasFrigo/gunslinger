# Feature Roadmap & Key Concepts: VR Gunslinger Game

Ordered cheapest → costliest for an agent to implement, given what already exists. Cost is the context a turn has to hold, how many live systems a wrong edit breaks, and how many sessions a miss takes. A stronger model plans the upper tiers; a cheaper model implements once that plan is settled. An item that names a dependency stays after it.

**Agent pick** on each item names the model and effort for the plan and the build. Tiers, cheapest first: Haiku 4.5 → Sonnet 5.5 → Opus 5.5 → Fable 5.1. Effort runs `low` → `medium` → `high` → `xhigh` → `max`. Raise effort before you raise the tier. Go up a tier when the work is physics or feel the agent cannot see, or when a wrong edit reaches combat resolve or netcode.

**Living status of what already ships:** `[docs/FEATURES.md](docs/FEATURES.md)` · **How systems work:** `[docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)` · **Bugs:** `[docs/BUGS.md](docs/BUGS.md)` · **Pre-release TODOs:** `[docs/RELEASE_TODOS.md](docs/RELEASE_TODOS.md)` · **SFX checklist:** `[docs/SOUND.md](docs/SOUND.md)` · **Design / lore:** `[docs/design/README.md](docs/design/README.md)` · **Versions:** `[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md)` / `[VERSION](VERSION)`

## 1. Low — small context

A cheaper model. One named file, or a catalog entry that already plays. Do not explore the repo.

1. **Shorter bullet trails:** Trails linger too long after the slug is gone. Drop `FADE_TIME` in `weapons/bullet_trail.gd` (currently 1.6s) so the ribbon disappears faster; optional debug knob. **Agent pick:** Haiku 4.5 (one constant).
2. **Replace placeholder SFX:** Combat cues play today via `PlaceholderAudio` except `duel_end.wav`. Check boxes in `[docs/SOUND.md](docs/SOUND.md)` as real files land in `assets/audio/`. Gunshot first. The cost is sourcing the audio, not code. **Agent pick:** Haiku 4.5 to wire each file once it exists.
3. **Barrel smoke:** Visible smoke coming out of the barrel after a shot. A short stub already plays (`VfxCatalog` `&"muzzle_smoke"` from `ImpactFeedback.shot_fired` / `assets/vfx/muzzle_smoke.tscn`); this is a lingering plume that reads as gunsmoke, not a 0.45s puff. One scene, but the look only shows in a screenshot or playtest, so expect a few tuning rounds. **Agent pick:** Sonnet 5.5 `medium`.



## 2. Moderate — one focused session

A mid-tier coding model. The spec is already tight, or another actor already does the thing. Name the files. A stronger model does not earn its keep here.

1. **Wind bed + gusts:** Always-on outdoor wind, mixed per arena, plus occasional visual gusts that fire a gust SFX at the same moment. Bed can start from `ScenarioResource.ambience` (`scenario_base.gd`) — unused today. Gusts are a new `VfxCatalog` cue (dust/tumbleweed/cloth) paired with an `AudioCatalog` `&"wind_gust"` one-shot. Per map: quiet/still Main Street, drier Canyon, more height/air on Train Rooftop; Saloon stays interior (no outdoor bed). Cosmetic only (local, no net). Foley notes in `[docs/SOUND.md](docs/SOUND.md)`. `scenario_base.gd` already plays `ambience` when it is set; no scenario `.tres` sets one yet. **Agent pick:** Sonnet 5.5 `medium`.
2. **Duel title cards:** Before the standoff, a Tekken-style intro isolates each duelist in turn. A fixed 3D camera points at them in the live arena, in whatever pose they are already holding; flat and VR both take that camera. A shared multilayer 2D card slams in from the side and holds about 2 seconds — one western template for every fighter, big letters are the name only. NPCs use `AIArchetype.display_name`. Humans use a display name in Settings; the Steam persona name overrides it while online. Order: in SP the NPC first, then the player; in 1v1 the host, then the joiner. Later 3+ modes cycle every combatant. You can walk, draw, and spin the revolver; fire stays locked. Fire or a button skips to the standoff, and in MP either peer skips both. A whoosh plays as the layers hit, plus a short sting per card. Plays on a new matchup in free duel, gauntlet, and 1v1; a rematch of that same duel skips it. Practice hub has no intro. The existing standoff → bell → draw still follows. The writeup is the plan; the cost is the shared camera, the card, and the skip RPC. This is the largest item in this tier: a new pre-standoff state in `gauntlet/duel_manager.gd`, a Settings name, the Steam persona, an MP skip RPC, rematch detection, and a fire lock. The VR camera can follow the spectator `XRCamera3D` swap that `autoload/kill_cam.gd` already does. **Agent pick:** Opus 5.5 `high` for one session. Sonnet 5.5 `high` is enough if you split it (SP card + camera first, MP skip second).



## 3. Very high — several sessions

The plan, and the part that is expensive to undo, stay on a stronger model. A mid-tier model can do the follow-through once a template exists.

1. **Mixed lobbies (up to 4 duelists):** Online lobbies of 2–4 duelists, any mix of real players and NPCs, on LAN (every SKU, Quest included) and Steam (desktop only). The host sets the roster: a slot count, and which empty slots are NPCs (an archetype or Mixed). The duel starts once every human slot is filled; a leaver's slot becomes an NPC from the next duel, and a human who drops mid-duel counts as dead with no ragdoll. The lobby is joinable only between duels. Modes: free-for-all, 2v2, and 1v3, with any human/NPC mix on each side. Friendly fire is on, as crossfire is in the SP free-for-all; the winner is the last side with anyone alive (a single duelist in free-for-all). The host runs the NPCs and clients see them as proxy avatars on the pose stream, so NPC poses and shots add bandwidth per NPC. Every human gets an avatar and a proximity voice player (`autoload/voice_chat.gd`); NPCs are silent. Builds on the shipped SP multi-NPC duel (shooter ids, N-actor ids, ring markers per arena, free-for-all targeting, last-standing win, N-actor replay and death cam). What stays 1v1 and must be generalized: the `DuelManager` MP RPCs (`_mp_finish`, `_mp_wound`, `mp_report_hit` take host/peer booleans and two health variables), the single `GameManager.remote_avatar` and the pose stream, the `ReplayBuffer` clip stride (`ACTORS`), the 2-player lobby caps on LAN and Steam, and the version check. Widening the replay stride changes the net protocol, so it needs a MINOR bump and is the part that is costly to undo; plan it before any RPC. A Quest frame-time check with 4 mannequins and 3 ragdolls is a release gate (cap the Quest lobby if it fails). Meta Store gets no non-LAN path. **Agent pick:** plan on Opus 5.5 `xhigh` (protocol, authority, team rules). Build the lobby and roster UI and the avatar fan-out on Sonnet 5.5 `high` once the RPC shape is fixed; do the replay stride and `DuelManager` resolve generalization on Opus 5.5 `high`.
2. **Mod Support:** Provide community modding capabilities (custom gun skins, custom maps, sound packs, and duel scenarios). Plan the loading boundary before any loader; a wrong API is costly to unwind. The plan also has to settle Quest (sideloaded content, store rules), whether mods may ship GDScript, and what the MP version check does with modded clients. **Agent pick:** plan on Fable 5.1 `high` (or Opus 5.5 `max`). Build each loader on Sonnet 5.5 `high` once the boundary is fixed.
3. **Campaign Mode:** Develop a narrative-driven or level-based single-player story mode. Highest session count here. The framework stays on a stronger model; a chapter is mid-tier work only after a template exists. **Agent pick:** plan the framework (progression, save, chapter format) on Opus 5.5 `xhigh`. Write chapters on Sonnet 5.5 `medium` once a template chapter exists. Story and lore can draw on `docs/design/`.



## Ideas

Not ready to build. These stay parked until you ask to mature one into a numbered section above. Add an item here only when asked. This list is not sorted by agent cost.

- **Airborne fire / mystic trick shots:** Optional tech/mystic branch: allow firing while the revolver is tossed and spinning, so you can go for mid-air trick shots. Today fire/reload require `held` (`weapons/weapon_base.gd`). Gate behind a flag so the grounded western default stays.
- **Shootable hat:** The hat is its own volume, separate from the head. A brim hit that misses the skull deals no damage and knocks the hat off along the shot as a rigid body, with a cloth thump. A ray that meets the head is still a headshot (`combat/combat_rules.gd`), and the hat comes off with that hit. The loose hat can be grabbed and seated back on the head (flat look + F, VR off-hand grip), then shot off again. It stays as it was through the death cam. The next standoff puts it on again. Every character that wears one — none do today; the live body is the hatless mannequin (`dummy.glb`). Blocked on the gunslinger outfit being worn in a duel. In 1v1 the host resolves the knockoff and the reseat so both peers agree.
- **More off-hand props:** Extra radial wedges beyond the cigarette, coin, ace, and bottle — badge, etc. Each needs a mesh vendored next to `assets/models/props/msc_cigarette.glb` and an entry in `PropController.ITEMS`; the wheel, attach, and stow plumbing already exist. Props are local-only today: remote avatars show nothing in the off hand, so MP pose sync (a flag plus the equipped id) is the other half. Cigarette polish that is still open: an ember emission / tiny light on the tip, and a readability pass on the thrown cig (real 8.4 cm scale is nearly invisible in flight — see `cig_spin_axis`).
- **More enemy NPCs:** Expand the roster beyond Drunk / Sheriff / Ghost. New opponents are new `ai/archetypes/*.tres` (`AIArchetype` — reaction, accuracy, draw, reload, move style). Wire them into the free-duel pick and gauntlet ladder rungs. Distinct silhouettes/meshes can follow once the gunslinger model is on AI. First design: [Half-head](docs/design/characters/half-head.md).
- **Train Map Concept:** A duel scene featuring a moving train passing between opponents. Players must either wait for the train to clear or attempt risky shots through open train cars. Train Rooftop already scrolls desert and horizon past a stopped train; this item is the train that crosses the lane.
- **Oil-field train:** A variant of the Train Map Concept. The same train crosses the lane, this time through open desert, with oil pumps (pumpjacks) scattered along both sides of the track. On the horizon, oil bursts burn as distant flame columns — a `VfxCatalog` cue, cosmetic only (local, no gameplay). Depends on the crossing train.
- **Severing (dismemberment):** A killing hit severs the whole head, an arm, or a leg and the piece rides the ragdoll (`characters/body_ragdoll.gd`). Scrapped for now in favor of hit chunks, which only knock a small patch out of the mesh. It would need pre-cut boolean meshes and stumps, keyed off the existing hitboxes. Cosmetic only; `combat/combat_rules.gd` stays as is.
- **Horseback duel stage:** Both duelists fight mounted. Each horse’s speed varies randomly, so relative motion (and the shot window) changes from duel to duel. New arena/scenario; riding locomotion + aim-on-the-move on top of the existing standoff.
- **Ranking & Leaderboard System:** Implement competitive online matchmaking, player ratings, and global/regional leaderboards on **Steam / desktop**. Not on the Meta Store SKU (LAN-only MP).

## Completed

- **Hit chunks v2 (cut where the bullet landed):** A hit cuts a 5–10 cm sphere (arms and legs capped at 6 cm) out of the skin at the real impact point, on any skinned mesh, with no mesh rebuild: a skin shader discards inside up to 8 bone-space holes per body (the ninth heals the oldest) and a cavity pass draws a red bowl through the cut. Hands are never cut. Each hit throws a procedural lumpy gib sized to the cut. Replay and 1v1 carry the cut (`DuelManager._mp_chunk(victim_is_host, bone, local, radius, dir, clip_t, lethal)`, net protocol change). `characters/body_skin.gdshader`, `characters/body_cavity.gdshader`, `characters/body_chunks.gd`, `characters/dummy_body.gd` (`cut_at`); `dev/cut_dummy_chunks.py` and the `Chunk_*` slots in `dummy.glb` are gone.
