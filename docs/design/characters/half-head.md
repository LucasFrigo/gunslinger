## Look

- **Head:** Half the skull exposed **horizontally** (top or bottom half bone, the other half flesh — not a left/right split). Wound stays readable at duel distance.
- **Clothes:** Long poncho, big black-and-white checker. The pattern is the body silhouette; do not mute it into dust-brown like the other duelists.
- **Weapons:** Two revolvers. Dual-wield; not a single-gun duelist.

## Personality / voice

- Teasing. Needles the player; enjoys the standoff.
- Visceral and violent. Mean in how he talks and how he kills.
- Voice: raspy and high-pitched.
