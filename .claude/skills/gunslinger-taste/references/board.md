The board changes; re-pull it with `scripts/fetch_cosmos.py` when the user says they added to it, or when this snapshot is more than a few weeks old, and add what is new here.

Numbers match the `NN` in the fetched file names. "Credit" is the account the image was saved from, which is often a reposter and not the maker; say "via" unless the maker is named.

## What the board keeps saying

Seven patterns repeat across the 32 images. An idea that uses none of them is probably off-board.

1. **The face is withheld.** Bandana over the whole head (25), beaded veil (24), luchador mask (23), bandages (28, 29), hat brim tipped over the eyes (10, 14), pure silhouette (04, 20), veil of cloth (22). Identity comes from the outfit and the outline. The Player page already does this.
2. **Colour is loud and few.** Two or three saturated flats per image: cobalt and magenta (28), orange and cornflower (25), blood red on red (24, 26), ox-blood and cream (04), cyan and white (14), indigo and black (20). Nothing on the board is dust-brown except the period photographs.
3. **Pattern is the silhouette.** Cowhide suit on a cowhide horse (13), houndstooth jacket (30), gingham shirt (14), chain-stitched yokes with arrows (11), rhinestone vines down a black suit (10), embroidered horses on an orange jacket (25), studs on red leather (26), striped polo wraps on a horse's legs (03). Half-head's checker poncho belongs here.
4. **Ornament on the tool.** Tiffany-decorated Smith & Wesson with a carved grip (06), engraved pocket revolvers (01), a skull-nosed revolver in the foreground of the Big Iron painting (00). The gun is jewellery and tells you who owns it.
5. **Horse and rider are one figure.** Polo posters (03, 18), the Mavericks letterpress (19), the two-gun rider at a gallop (27), the pink shroud on a rearing white horse (22), Araki's New Year card (31). The horse carries colour and pattern too.
6. **Fashion pose over action pose.** Bodies are bent, leaning, hand to hat, asleep, or simply standing (02, 10, 11, 16, 24). Stillness and attitude read as threat. Only 08 and 27 are shooting.
7. **Print texture.** Halftone dots on orange (15), letterpress misregistration (19), flat litho inks (03), albumen sepia (05, 07), film grain (04, 12). The reference is the printed West, not the filmed one.
